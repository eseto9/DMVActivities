# Weekly update checklist

What the automatic update does every **Wednesday at 7 PM** for the Date Nights in the DMV calendar. It only runs if your weekly Claude plan usage is at **80% or below**. It ends by committing and pushing to GitHub.

## 1. Before anything else
- Check weekly plan usage; skip the whole run if it's above 80%.
- Pull the latest version from GitHub. Stop if there are unsaved local changes.

## 2. Re-check what's already listed
- Re-open every upcoming event's source page and confirm the **date, time and price** still match. Fix anything that changed.
- Remove events that were cancelled, sold out, or are already over. Multi-day runs with some dates left get "Sold out" noted on the price.
- Re-check events with an estimated price ("Est."). Swap in the real price once it's published.
- Re-check 5–8 **Any day** places (rotating) for hours and prices. Remove anything that closed.

## 3. Find new events in every area

Every run searches **all nine areas**, going source by source through each area's own local calendars. Your two home areas come first, with at least 4 new events there each week. That source-by-source method is what found the Tysons events that broad searches missed.

### Eventbrite sweep
Eventbrite's search pages get swept for food festivals, festivals, fairs, markets and food tours in DC, Baltimore, Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria, Columbia, Rockville, Sterling and National Harbor. Each event page is then checked for exact time, price and sold-out status.

### Kinds of events it looks for
- **Most wanted:** food festivals, restaurant weeks, fairs, expos, makers' markets, Wizards and Capitals home games
- **Chef events:** guest-chef dinners, tasting menus, chef collaborations
- **Trivia and game nights:** board-game cafés, escape rooms, murder-mystery dinners
- **Classes and workshops (not cooking):** pottery, glass, candles, flowers, painting, jewelry, couples dance lessons
- **Outdoors in winter:** night and full-moon hikes, ranger walks, eagle watching, stargazing, bonfires, first-day hikes, ice rinks
- **Holiday:** light shows, lightings, boat parades, candlelight house tours
- **Culture:** museum late nights and free days, planetarium nights, art walks, comedy
- **Seasonal:** Lunar New Year, Valentine's, Mardi Gras, St. Patrick's parades, cherry blossom and other spring previews
- **Volunteering:** dated shifts you can do together, like food banks, DC Central Kitchen, park cleanups, Wreaths Across America and MLK Day of Service. Keeps 4–8 on the calendar.
- **Other:** arcades, duckpin, bowling, mini golf, roller skating, outdoor movies, harbor cruises, flower shows, arena shows like Monster Jam

### Never added
- Events where alcohol is the main thing: beer, wine or spirits festivals, bar crawls, brewery or distillery tours, tastings. Drinks mentioned in passing are fine.
- Meditation and wellness
- Drag shows
- Running races: 5Ks, marathons, turkey trots, fun runs
- Cooking classes
- Live-music concerts
- Theater, musicals, ballet and opera
- Kid-focused events
- Basketball other than the Wizards

### What every new event needs
- A confirmed **date, clock time and price** (or "Free") from the official or ticketing page. Big events without a published price can go in with a gold "Est." price.
- A **real photo**: never Eventbrite's flyer image. The venue's or organizer's own photo comes first, then a Wikimedia Commons photo of the venue, park or neighborhood.
- A **crowd estimate**, which sets the sort order.
- A town the page recognizes, so the area filter works.
- An **outdoors** flag if it's mainly outside, so rainy days can warn about it and move indoor events up.
- A **Sells out** tag only for events that really do sell out or fill up, like timed-entry light shows, candlelight tours, owl prowls and headline comedians.
- A **can't-miss** flag only if it truly is one. The Coming up row holds about 15–25 at a time.

## 4. Any day, Movies and Cheap movie Tuesday
- **Any day:** add 2–4 new places each week with confirmed hours, price and a typical-day crowd estimate. Idea sources include the DC Hotspots "DC Date Ideas (A–Z)" pages, Washingtonian's "50 Great Date Ideas" and the Date Nights DC hub.
- **Movies:** rebuild as the top 10 in theaters from last weekend's box office (Box Office Mojo, or The Numbers as backup), plus notable films opening in the next ~10 weeks. Each gets its Wikipedia poster and an estimated date it leaves theaters.
- **Cheap movie Tuesday:** once a month, re-check each chain's Tuesday deal and holiday blackouts. That covers LOOK Dine-In, Landmark, Warehouse Cinemas, AMC, Regal, Paragon Founders Row, University Mall, Alamo and Cinemark, with theaters near Rockville, Bethesda and Tysons listed first.

## 5. Photos
- Every event, place and movie needs a photo, with no Eventbrite flyer images.
- Wizards and Capitals games use the opponent's team logo.
- Up to 10 broken images get fixed each week.

## 6. Check, publish and report
- Keep the calendar covering about 4 months ahead and drop past months.
- Update the "Checked" date in the footer.
- Make sure the page loads with no errors, then commit and push to GitHub. It never force-pushes, and it skips the commit if nothing changed.
- Report back with:
  - usage at the start
  - a table of added events
  - what changed or was removed
  - anything skipped and why
  - per-area counts
  - whether the push worked
